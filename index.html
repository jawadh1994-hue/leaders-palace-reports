Leaders Palace Reports

Leaders Palace Reports is a private, internal tool used by Leaders Palace to read its own daily point-of-sale report emails from the gentsleaderspalace@gmail.com mailbox, so they can be summarised by an AI assistant running on the business's own computer.

It is not offered to the public and is only used with Leaders Palace's own Google account.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What data the app accesses. With the account owner's permission, the app requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It searches for and reads emails and PDF attachments, mainly the daily sales reports.

How the data is used. Email content is used only to produce reports and summaries for Leaders Palace's own business use. The app cannot send, delete or change emails.

Storage. The sign-in token is stored only on Leaders Palace's own computer. The app does not run its own server and does not keep a separate copy of emails.

Sharing. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising. Email content may be processed by the AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) that the business uses to prepare its reports.

Google API Services. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
