Leaders Palace Reports

Leaders Palace Reports is a private, internal tool used by Leaders Palace to read its own daily point-of-sale report emails from the gentsleaderspalace@gmail.com mailbox, summarise them with an AI assistant running on the business's own computer, and record and reconcile the figures in the business's own Google Sheets.

It is not offered to the public and is only used with Leaders Palace's own Google account.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What data the app accesses. With the account owner's permission, the app requests:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): it searches for and reads emails and PDF attachments, mainly the daily card-payment reports. It cannot send, delete or change emails.
  • Google Sheets, read and write (spreadsheets): it reads Leaders Palace's own financial sheets and creates and updates a payments tracker sheet, including marking differences between recorded and actual payments.
  • Google Drive, app files only (drive.file): it can only see and organise the files it creates itself (the new monthly financial sheet), to place them in the business's folder. It cannot see any other Drive files.
  • Google Calendar, events (calendar.events): it adds each booking made on the Leaders Palace website as an event in the business's own calendar.

How the data is used. Email and sheet content is used only to produce reports, summaries and reconciliations for Leaders Palace's own business use.

Storage. The sign-in token, and the payment figures extracted from the reports, are stored only on Leaders Palace's own computer and in the business's own Google Sheets. The app does not run its own server.

Sharing. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising. Email content may be processed by the AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) that the business uses to prepare its reports.

Google API Services. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
